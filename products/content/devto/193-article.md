Analyze any text for readability metrics. POST to https://toolpipe.dev/text/analyze. Returns Flesch-Kincaid score, word count, reading time, sentence count, and more. Perfect for content optimization and SEO writing. Free, no API key needed.

All tools available at [toolpipe.dev](https://toolpipe.dev). Also available as an MCP server for AI agents: `npx @cosai-labs/toolpipe-mcp-server`
