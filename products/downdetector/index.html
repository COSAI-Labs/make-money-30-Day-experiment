Free Website Down Checker

Is a website down, or is it just your connection? Our free tool checks any website's status from our servers, so you can tell if the problem is on your end or theirs.

How It Works

Enter any URL and we'll attempt to connect to it from our server. We check the HTTP response code, measure response time, and resolve the domain's IP address. If the site responds with a 2xx or 3xx status code, it's up. If we get a timeout, connection error, or 5xx error, it's likely down.

Common Reasons Websites Go Down

Server overload, DNS issues, expired SSL certificates, DDoS attacks, hosting provider outages, or scheduled maintenance. If a site is down for everyone, the issue is on their end. If it's just you, try clearing your DNS cache, checking your internet connection, or using a VPN.
