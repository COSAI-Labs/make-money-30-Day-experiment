Base58 Encoder/Decoder

Encode and decode Base58 strings (used in Bitcoin/crypto)

What is Base58?

Base58 is a binary-to-text encoding used in Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies. It is similar to Base64 but excludes confusing characters like 0, O, I, and l.
