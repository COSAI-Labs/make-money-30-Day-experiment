What is a Character Counter?

A character counter tells you exactly how many characters, words, sentences, and paragraphs are in your text. Essential for Twitter posts (280 chars), meta descriptions (160 chars), essays, and more.

Why Use This Tool?

Perfect for social media posts, SEO meta descriptions, academic essays with word limits, SMS messages, and any content that needs to fit within specific character or word constraints.
