Color Palette Generator

Create harmonious color schemes from any base color

Color Harmony Theory

Color harmonies are combinations of colors that are visually pleasing. They are based on the color wheel, where colors are arranged by hue. The most common harmonies are complementary (opposite colors), analogous (adjacent colors), and triadic (three evenly spaced colors).

How to Use This Tool

Pick a base color using the color picker or enter a hex code. Choose a harmony type from the dropdown. The palette will generate automatically. Click any swatch to copy its hex value. Use "Copy CSS Variables" to get ready-to-use CSS custom properties.

Choosing the Right Palette

Complementary palettes create high contrast and energy. Analogous palettes feel calm and cohesive. Triadic palettes are vibrant and balanced. Monochromatic palettes are elegant and easy to manage. Split-complementary offers contrast without the tension of a full complement.
