Color Formats

HEX: A 6-digit hexadecimal representation. Example: #6c63ff. Each pair represents Red, Green, Blue (00-FF).

RGB: Red, Green, Blue values from 0-255. Example: rgb(108, 99, 255).

HSL: Hue (0-360), Saturation (0-100%), Lightness (0-100%). More intuitive for color manipulation.

WCAG Contrast Guidelines

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) require a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text (AA) and 7:1 for enhanced contrast (AAA). Large text requires 3:1 (AA) and 4.5:1 (AAA).
