How PDF Compression Works

Our tool compresses PDF content streams using standard PDF compression algorithms. This removes redundant data and optimizes the internal structure of the PDF without altering the visible content. The result is a smaller file that looks identical to the original.

Compression effectiveness varies by document type. PDFs with lots of images see the biggest reductions (50-80%), while text-heavy documents may see smaller gains (10-30%).
