    Cron Expression Format

    A cron expression is a string of 5 fields separated by spaces, representing a schedule:

    FieldValuesSpecial Characters
    Minute0-59* , - /
    Hour0-23* , - /
    Day of Month1-31* , - /
    Month1-12* , - /
    Day of Week0-6 (Sun=0)* , - /

    Special Characters

    CharacterMeaningExample
    *Any value* * * * * = every minute
    ,List1,15 * * * * = minute 1 and 15
    -Range1-5 * * * * = minutes 1 through 5
    /Step*/10 * * * * = every 10 minutes

    Common Examples

    ExpressionDescription
    0 0 * * *Every day at midnight
    */5 * * * *Every 5 minutes
    0 9 * * 1-5Weekdays at 9:00 AM
    0 0 1 * *First day of every month at midnight
    30 4 * * 0Every Sunday at 4:30 AM
    0 */2 * * *Every 2 hours
