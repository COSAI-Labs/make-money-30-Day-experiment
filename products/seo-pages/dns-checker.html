What is DNS?

DNS (Domain Name System) translates domain names (like example.com) into IP addresses that computers use to communicate. DNS records include A (IPv4), AAAA (IPv6), MX (mail servers), CNAME (aliases), NS (nameservers), TXT (text records), and SOA (authority).
