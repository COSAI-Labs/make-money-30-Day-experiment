Lorem Ipsum Generator

Generate placeholder text for your designs and layouts

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy text used in the printing and typesetting industry since the 1500s. It is used as placeholder content when the actual text is not yet available, allowing designers and developers to focus on layout and visual design without being distracted by readable content.

How to Use This Generator

Use the slider or number input to choose how many paragraphs you need (1 to 50). Select "Plain Text" for raw text or "HTML Paragraphs" to get text wrapped in paragraph tags. Click "Generate" and then "Copy" to paste it into your project.

API Access

Generate lorem ipsum programmatically: GET /api/lorem?paragraphs=5. Returns generated placeholder text in your chosen format. Perfect for automated content generation in development workflows.
