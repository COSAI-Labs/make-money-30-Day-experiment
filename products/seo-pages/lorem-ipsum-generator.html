What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy text used in the printing and typesetting industry since the 1500s. It is used as placeholder text to demonstrate the visual form of a document without relying on meaningful content.

Why Use Lorem Ipsum?

Designers and developers use Lorem Ipsum to fill layouts with text that has a natural distribution of letters, making it more realistic than repeating "content here." It helps stakeholders focus on design rather than content during reviews.

Origin

Lorem Ipsum comes from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (The Extremes of Good and Evil) by Cicero, written in 45 BC. Richard McClintock, a Latin professor, discovered the source in the 1960s.
