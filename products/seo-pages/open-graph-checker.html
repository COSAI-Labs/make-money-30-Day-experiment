What is Open Graph?

Open Graph (OG) is a protocol created by Facebook that controls how URLs are displayed when shared on social media. OG tags like og:title, og:description, and og:image determine the title, description, and preview image shown in social media cards on Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, and more.
