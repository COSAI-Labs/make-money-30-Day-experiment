Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up to use the free tier?

No. The free tier requires no account, no API key, and no credit card. Just start making requests or use the browser tools directly.

What happens if I exceed my daily API limit?

Requests beyond your daily limit will receive a 429 rate limit response. You can upgrade your plan at any time to increase your limit.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime from your dashboard.
