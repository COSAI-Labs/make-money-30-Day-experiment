What Can You Put in a QR Code?

  • Website URLs - link to any webpage
  • Plain text - messages, notes, instructions
  • WiFi credentials - format: WIFI:T:WPA;S:NetworkName;P:Password;;
  • Contact cards - vCard format for adding contacts
  • Email addresses - mailto: links
  • Phone numbers - tel: links for click-to-call

About Error Correction

QR codes include error correction that allows them to be read even when partially damaged. Higher levels mean more redundancy (the code still works if part is obscured), but also a denser pattern. Medium (15%) is a good default for most uses.

FAQ

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. Generate as many QR codes as you need.

Do QR codes expire?

No. QR codes are just encoded data. They work forever as long as the content they point to (like a URL) is still active.

What size should I use?

300x300 is good for digital use. For print (posters, business cards), use 800x800 or higher.
