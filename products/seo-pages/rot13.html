ROT13 Encoder/Decoder

Encode or decode text using the ROT13 cipher

What is ROT13?

ROT13 is a simple letter substitution cipher that replaces each letter with the letter 13 positions after it in the alphabet. It is its own inverse: applying ROT13 twice returns the original text.
