What is a URL Slug?

A URL slug is the part of a URL that identifies a particular page in a human-readable form. For example, in "example.com/my-blog-post", "my-blog-post" is the slug.

SEO Benefits

Clean URL slugs improve SEO by making URLs readable and descriptive. Search engines prefer short, keyword-rich URLs that describe the page content.
