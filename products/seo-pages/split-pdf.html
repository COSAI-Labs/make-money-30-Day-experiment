How to Split a PDF

  • Step 1: Upload your PDF file (max 50MB).
  • Step 2: Enter the page numbers to extract (e.g., 1-3, 5, 7-10).
  • Step 3: Click "Split PDF" and download your extracted pages.

Page Range Examples

  • 1-5 extracts pages 1 through 5
  • 3, 7, 12 extracts only pages 3, 7, and 12
  • 1-3, 8-10 extracts pages 1 to 3 and 8 to 10
  • 5 extracts just page 5
