Twitter Character Limits

Twitter (now X) has a 280-character limit for standard posts. URLs count as 23 characters regardless of length. Images and videos do not count toward the character limit.

Tips for Better Tweets

Keep tweets concise and engaging. Use hashtags sparingly (1-2 per tweet). Include a call to action. Ask questions to boost engagement.
