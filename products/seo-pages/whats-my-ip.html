What is an IP Address?

An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique numerical label assigned to every device connected to the internet. It serves as your device's identifier on the network, allowing data to be routed to and from your device. Your IP address reveals your approximate location and ISP.

Public vs Private IP

The IP shown above is your public IP, which is visible to websites and services you connect to. Your private IP (like 192.168.x.x) is only used within your local network and is not shown here.

Why Does My IP Matter?

Your IP address can reveal your approximate location, ISP, and can be used for geolocation, content delivery optimization, and security. Using a VPN can mask your real IP address for privacy.
