Free URL Shortener with Analytics

ToolPipe URL Shortener creates short, shareable links with built-in click tracking. See how many people click your links, where they come from, and when they visit. All for free, no account needed.

Features

Custom aliases: Choose your own short code instead of a random string. Make links memorable and branded.

Click analytics: Track total clicks, daily trends, and referrer sources. Know exactly how your links perform.

No signup: Create short links instantly. No email, no account, no friction.

API access: Programmatically create and manage short URLs via our REST API.
